# Early access

## Getting started

Follow these steps to join early access and start studying.

1. **Download the app**
   - **iOS** — install via [TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/46KgYyth). You need the TestFlight app from the App Store, then accept the Rete beta invite.
   - **Android** — Google Play is on internal testing, so you are not added automatically. [Contact us](/contact) with your Google account email and ask to be put on the invite list. After you accept the invite, install Rete from Google Play.

2. **Register an account** — open the app and create a username, email, and password (or sign in if you already have an account). See [Login / Registration](/guide/get-started/login-registration) for details.

3. **Find a study deck** — open the **Discover** tab, browse or search public decks, import one you want to study, then start reviewing from your deck list.

For product background, see [What is Rete](/guide/background/what-is-retentio). For creating your own deck, see [Create your deck](/guide/get-started/create-your-deck) and [Start studying](/guide/get-started/start-studying).

Thank you for trying Rete early. The product is **in active development**: features ship continuously, some areas are incomplete, and behavior can change as we learn from real study sessions.

This page also sets expectations for early access and explains how you can help.

## Professional audio — listen, then speak

Rete uses the **most cutting-edge text-to-speech models** to generate **professional-sounding** audio for your cards — clear, natural pronunciation you can trust, not muffled or robotic clips.

When you review, **play the audio, then repeat it out loud**. That practice — called **vocalization** or **shadowing** — is one of the most effective techniques in language acquisition. By echoing professional speech right after you hear it, you train your ear and mouth together and pick up a more natural, professional accent over time.

## What early access means

- **Still building.** Core flows — decks, facts, cards, and review — work today, but many parts of the product are unfinished or will be redesigned.
- **Things will change.** Screens, workflows, and defaults may shift. We aim to avoid needless disruption, but breaking changes and rough edges are normal at this stage.
- **Bugs and gaps are expected.** You may hit errors, missing polish, incomplete translations, or features that look ready but are only partly wired. Please report what you find rather than assuming someone else already did.
- **Your feedback matters.** Early access works only if we hear what helps learning — and what gets in the way. Specific examples beat vague impressions.

## What we are optimizing for

Rete is built around **long-term retention** and sustainable daily review, not engagement theater. While the UI and tooling still evolve, that priority stays fixed: make spaced repetition simpler to start, easier to keep up with, and better for shared deck quality over time.

If something feels confusing, slow, or wrong for studying, that is useful signal — not something you should “work around” quietly.

## How to give feedback

The most useful reports include:

1. **What you were trying to do** (e.g. create a deck, import cards, finish a review session)
2. **What happened** (unexpected UI, error message, wrong schedule, missing content)
3. **What you expected** instead
4. **Context** when you can: browser or device, locale, and roughly when it occurred

Send feedback through [Contact](/contact) — email reaches the team directly. Feature ideas, usability friction, content quality issues, and bugs are all welcome.
