# Login and Register

Login and Register are the entry points to your Rete account. **Login** restores access to your decks, facts, cards, and review progress. **Register** creates a new account so you can import shared decks, build your own material, and study.

Learning data and schedules are tied to your account, so signing in keeps your work available across sessions and devices.

## Login

Login uses **username** and **password**. When they match, you reach the main app — **Decks**, **Discover**, and **Profile**.

If credentials are wrong, check the username and password and try again.

From the login screen you can open **Register** or **Forgot Password?**.

## Register

Register creates a new account. Provide:

- **Email** — account contact and email verification
- **Username** — how you sign in
- **Password** and **Confirm Password** — must match; password must be at least **8** characters

On the **website**, you must also accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy before registering.

After a successful registration, the app asks you to **check your email** to verify the address, then returns you to login. Sign in with your new username and password to continue.

## Email verification

Verification is **recommended but not required** to use Rete. You can study while unverified. If your email is not verified yet, **Profile** shows a reminder and a **Resend verification email** action. Opening the link in the email (or the in-app verify screen) confirms the address.

## Password recovery

If you cannot sign in:

1. On login, tap **Forgot Password?**
2. Enter the **email** on the account
3. If an account exists, a reset link is sent
4. Open the link, set a **new password** (and confirm it), then sign in again

Your decks and progress stay on the account after you reset the password.

## Switching between login and register

Login links to Register, and Register links back to Login, so new and returning users can reach the right flow quickly.

## Summary

Login restores an existing account; Register creates one with email, username, and password. Verify email when you can, use Forgot Password if you get locked out, then move on to [import a shared deck](/guide/get-started/import-an-existing-deck) or [create your own](/guide/get-started/create-your-deck).
