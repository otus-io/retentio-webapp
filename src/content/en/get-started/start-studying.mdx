# Start Studying

Once your deck has facts and cards — whether you [imported](/guide/get-started/import-an-existing-deck) or [created](/guide/get-started/create-your-deck) it — open the deck from the **Decks** tab and begin review. Rete picks the most urgent due card — optionally filtered by [tag](/guide/key-concepts/tags) — and walks you through **recall**, **show answer**, and **rate difficulty**.

## See the front and recall

The card shows its **front** first: one or more **entries** from the fact (often with deck field labels, such as `English:`).

Example:

`English: hello`

Try to recall the answer before revealing the back — for example `こんにちは`. Active recall matters more than recognizing text you have seen before.

The front can also include images, audio, or video, depending on the entries and card layout.

Progress for the session appears as **Today's due** so you can see how much of today’s target you have cleared.

## Show answer

When you are ready, tap the card or the bottom button **Show Answer**.

The **back** appears so you can check your recall:

Front: `hello`  
Back: `こんにちは`

If you remembered well, the fact has a stronger base; if not, it will come back sooner in review.

## Hard or easy

After the answer is visible, use the **difficulty slider** at the bottom. **Hard** is on the left, **Easy** on the right — slide to match how the recall actually felt.

- Barely remembered or struggled → toward **Hard**
- Quick, accurate recall → toward **Easy**

Choose honestly. Random ratings throw off scheduling.

Tap **Next** to save your rating and load the next card. The algorithm uses your feedback to set when that card appears again: harder cards sooner, easier cards later.

## Tag filter

If the deck has tags, use the filter chips (**All** or a specific tag) to narrow which cards enter review. Clear the filter when you want the full queue again.

## The study loop

Each card follows the same loop:

1. Read the **front** and recall the answer
2. **Show Answer** and compare
3. Rate **Hard** or **Easy**, then tap **Next**

When you finish the due set, you may see **All caught up!** — keep a steady daily habit rather than cramming.

## Summary

**Start studying** is where spaced repetition happens: front → show answer → difficulty → **Next**. Get content via [Discover import](/guide/get-started/import-an-existing-deck) or [Create your deck](/guide/get-started/create-your-deck); use this page for the daily review flow.
