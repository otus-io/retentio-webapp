# Install on Android

Rete for Android is in testing on Google Play. Use the **same Google account** for all three steps.

1. **Join the group** — open [rete-learning on Google Groups](https://groups.google.com/g/rete-learning) and click **Join group**. Group members can become testers.
2. **Become a tester** — open the [testing page](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/srs.app.retentio) and tap **Become a tester**. If it says the app is unavailable, wait a few minutes after joining the group and reload.
3. **Install** — open [Rete on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=srs.app.retentio) and tap **Install**. Updates arrive through Google Play like any other app.

Can't use Google Play? [Download the APK](https://github.com/otus-io/retentio-frontend/releases/latest/download/retentio.apk) instead.

Once installed, [register or sign in](/guide/get-started/login-registration).
