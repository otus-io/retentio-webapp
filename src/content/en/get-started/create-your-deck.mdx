# Create Your Deck

After you [log in](/guide/get-started/login-registration), you can build your own study material: create a **deck**, add **facts**, and let Rete generate **cards** for review. For how decks, facts, and cards relate, see [Key concepts](/guide/key-concepts/overview).

If you want ready-made material first, [import a shared deck from Discover](/guide/get-started/import-an-existing-deck) instead.

## Create a deck

Open the **Decks** tab and tap the **Create Deck** button (floating action button) to open the create sheet.

### Rate (new cards per day)

Choose how many **new cards** to introduce per day (`cards per day`). That sets how much fresh material enters your review queue each day — pick a value that matches the time you can study. Higher values introduce new material faster; lower values keep a steadier pace.

### Deck name

The deck name identifies the deck in your library. Enter it in the create sheet.

### Description

**Description** is required. People read it on the deck in **Discover**. It can be up to **500** characters. On an imported deck the description follows the author and cannot be edited.

### Tags

Optionally add **tags** when you create the deck so you can filter study later. See [Tags](/guide/key-concepts/tags).

### Fields (columns)

Define **fields** (column names) — for example `English`, `Japanese`, `Example Sentence`, or `Note`. Field names live on the deck; they label each column when you edit and study.

By default you get **2** fields; you can reorder columns and **Add field** for more. Facts fill **entries** in the same order as those columns. See [Decks](/guide/key-concepts/decks).

Tap **Save**. The new deck appears in your library — open it to add content.

## Add facts

Inside the deck, add **facts**: one complete item to remember (word pair, term + definition, and so on). Each fact stores **entries** in column order — not field names on the fact itself.

Example on a deck with fields `English` and `Japanese` — one row is one fact:

| English | Japanese   |
| ------- | ---------- |
| hello   | こんにちは |

With more columns you add more entries (example sentences, images, audio, notes). Details are in [Facts](/guide/key-concepts/facts).

## Generate cards

When you add a fact, the system creates **cards** from its entries.

**By default**, one fact yields one card: front = first entry, back = the rest.

Front: `hello`  
Back: `こんにちは`

One fact can also produce **sibling** cards — for example English→Japanese and Japanese→English. See [Cards](/guide/key-concepts/cards).

## Publish your deck

Publishing shares a **snapshot** of the deck, not a live link to your working copy. You can keep editing after you publish. Friends only see those edits after you publish again.

```
Your working deck  →  Publish Deck  →  public snapshot v1
                                            ↓
                          a friend imports  →  their study copy (pinned to v1)
```

Publishing, and sending a correction to someone else, needs a [verified email](/guide/get-started/login-registration#email-verification).

### First publish

When the deck is ready for other people:

1. Open the deck **⋯** menu, or open the deck’s detail page.
2. Choose **Publish Deck**.

The deck then appears in **Discover** as **Public**, at version **v1**.

That first publish stays in effect:

- The deck remains public. You cannot make it private again.
- You cannot delete a published deck.

Each friend who imports it gets their own cards, due dates, and progress. They do not see your review history, and they do not edit your working copy directly.

### Publish an update

Changes you make after publishing stay on your working copy until you publish them.

1. Edit facts, media, tags, or the deck itself as usual.
2. Open **Update Published Version** from the deck **⋯** menu. When something is unpublished, the share icon on the deck card opens the same sheet.
3. Read the preview: facts added, edited, or removed, plus media, tag, and template changes.
4. Publish. That creates the next snapshot (`v2`, `v3`, and so on).

If nothing changed since the last version, there is nothing new to publish. An imported copy stays on the version that person already has until they choose to update.

## Work on a deck with friends

Friends do not share one live deck. They import your published snapshot, study on their own copy, and send **contributions** when a correction or a new fact should go into the source. You choose what to keep, then publish an update so everyone else can receive it.

### Correct or add something

On the imported deck:

- **Edit Fact** (card **⋯**) — fix an entry, example, or media. Saving updates their copy and sends the correction to you.
- **Add fact** (deck **⋯**) — propose a new fact the same way.
- **Report issue** (card **⋯**) — flag audio, content, or something else. This is sent immediately and does not change the fact.

If a send cannot go through, the change waits in **Unsent contributions** (deck **⋯**). From there they can add an optional note, then **Send selected** or **Dismiss selected**. The same sheet can **Suggest field names** or **Suggest deck tags**.

Ratings, hidden cards, and due dates stay on their copy. Those are never sent.

### Review what friends sent

On your published deck, open **Contributions** from the deck **⋯** menu. When something is waiting, the inbox icon on the deck card opens the same list. Each item shows the change: before and after for an edit, or the proposed fact, fields, or tags.

| Action         | What it does                                                                          |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Accept**     | Writes the suggestion into your working copy                                          |
| **Dismiss**    | Drops it                                                                              |
| **Resolve**    | Closes it without applying the change. Use this for a report you have already handled |
| **Accept all** | Accepts every open suggestion. Reports are skipped                                    |

Accepting does not update Discover. When you want importers to receive those changes, publish again with **Update Published Version**.

### Take the new version

An import stays on its current version until that person updates. See also [Import an existing deck](/guide/get-started/import-an-existing-deck).

1. Open **Check updates** from the deck **⋯** menu, or the refresh icon on the deck card.
2. Review added, edited, and removed facts.
3. For a fact they also changed locally, choose **Accept** (take the published wording) or **Reject** (keep theirs).
4. Tap **Sync now**.

After sync, removed facts drop out and new facts get new cards. Review history stays for cards whose fact did not change.

## What’s next

When your deck has facts and cards, [start studying](/guide/get-started/start-studying): recall the front, show the answer, rate difficulty, then tap **Next**.
